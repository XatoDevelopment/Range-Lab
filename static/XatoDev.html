Projects

What I've built

Two very different projects: a poker study suite and a point-of-sale system for businesses.

RangeLab Suite

Available
FlaskSQLitePWADocker

Poker study suite with eight tools: Monte Carlo equity, preflop ranges, bankroll control, hand history review, tournament analysis and practice against bots. It runs on your own server, in English and Spanish, and can be installed as an app.

  • Flask application with SQLite, per-user data and login lockout after failed attempts.
  • Hand history import for CoinPoker, PokerStars and ACR, using my own parsers.
  • PWA with a service worker for offline use, and Docker deployment.
  • Source available under a non-commercial license.

XatoPos

In development
FlaskSQLiteJavaScriptRoles

Point-of-sale system designed for small and medium businesses. It manages sales, inventory, profits and expenses in one place, with clear reports to track how the business is doing.

  • Point of sale with cart, checkout and receipt generation.
  • Inventory management: stock, low-stock alerts and stock intake.
  • Profit and expense tracking with summaries by period.
  • User roles so each employee has the permissions they need.

This project is in development. It is not yet available for general download.

About me

A developer who finishes what he starts

I'm XatoDev, an independent developer. I work alone on my projects: design, backend, interface, testing and documentation.

I want to build things that really work: fast, clear and honest about their limits. That's why I document every project and publish the code when I can.

I'm open to collaborating with other people and to custom projects.

  • I finish it. Every project reaches a usable, documented state.
  • I test it. Automated tests and checks on different devices.
  • I explain it. Clear documentation for users and for anyone who wants to contribute.
  • Bilingual. I work and publish in English and Spanish.
